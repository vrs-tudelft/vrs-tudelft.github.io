Virtual Radiosonde

The whole project

Three parts, each built by part of the team, hand over to each other: the air near the ground is rebuilt from balloons, stations and a weather model; the simulation turns a known atmosphere into fake radar data; the estimation turns radar data, fake or real, back into the air.
