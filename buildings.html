Why height matters

Sinking moves both points together and cancels in their height contrast. Shrinking and thermal dilation grow with height and can imitate the air.

Model and data are different views

The model uses synthetic point heights and public weather. The observed views contain only radar aggregates. Neither is a measurement of the urban vertical atmosphere.

Parameter estimation

The report compares motion per building with per-scatterer trends. Temperature-correlated atmosphere can be confounded with the thermal response.
