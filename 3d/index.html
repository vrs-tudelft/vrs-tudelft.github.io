Height support is necessary, not sufficient

Move the height-band slider to see which buildings have a published aggregate there. Select a building for its point counts by height and its upper-minus-lower radar series.

Grey includes missing or suppressed groups. A coloured 10 m slab does not mean the entire facade is continuously observed. Geometry above the retained radar heights stays grey.

Read the colours

Counts, rates and seasonal values are published building, height-slab or grid-cell aggregates with at least 20 radar points. Supported height alone does not establish an independently recoverable air layer.

Rates are relative to the selected reference. They do not independently identify building motion or atmospheric delay.

Navigate the scene

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and select a building or cell for its aggregate values. The pass slider shows changes over time.
