Temperature can imitate air

The building and the atmosphere can both track temperature and height. A building correction can remove real atmospheric variation or leave a thermal residual.

No collocated vertical truth

Station agreement is a boundary check. Neither an air prior nor a weather-model column establishes a measured validation of the urban profile.
