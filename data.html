Observations and models

KNMI station 344 supplies the surface boundary. Radiosondes and the Cabauw tower provide independent observations at other locations and times.

Rotterdam weather-model columns are model output, not collocated profile measurements.

The shared ground reference

The median of 499 shared stable ground points sets each pass's zero. Report movements are relative to that reference, not absolute motion.

Heights are part of the model

Layer boundaries are metres NAP. Building assignment uses the supplied 3DBAG-based grouping; vertical datum and roof support matter when comparing datasets.

Public-data rule

Publish radar means over at least 20 points with their spread. No individual point position or time series is public.

Weather, building and source licences remain attached to their products.

Reproducibility

The private report bundle preserves exact inputs, code revisions and outputs with SHA256. The original frozen experiment remains distinct from the newer corrected weather archive.
